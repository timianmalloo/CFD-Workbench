**Recurrence control for partial streams:** After a native process exits, `python3 tools/coord-stream-summary.py <closed-jsonl> --require-completed-tool <name> --require-success-command <exact-command>` parses every row, hashes the full file, matches calls to final updates, and fails when a required completed tool or successful command is absent. On the final 117-row Grok receipt, `--require-success-command pwd` passed; `--require-success-command 'dotnet build'` failed. On the early 60-row prefix, requiring `run_terminal_command` failed. This prevents an early thought-only prefix from being mistaken for a final operation receipt. The process-exit receipt and filesystem diff still require separate checks.
