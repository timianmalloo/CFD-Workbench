## Rulings on the request

- **(a) The missing L3 rate:** "Not recorded, bounded". The inventory ran 4.05 s (`capture.json:16-17`). The
  pre-inventory L3 rate was 0.720 it/s (`win-r151-l3/capture.stdout.txt:37,44`), cross-checked at 0.718 it/s, so the
  window overlaps under 3 iterations. The record stays "Not recorded", not "no impact".
- **(b) The observer is the PC coordinator seat, never the GPU track.**
  - **Folder:** a neutral `docs/proof/win-l3-observer/`.
  - **Script:** `observe.sh`, invoked as a file through WSL. It reads only:
    - `date -u` and `/proc/uptime`;
    - `/proc/loadavg`;
    - the unit's ActiveState;
    - the bounded-tail iteration awk from `readback.sh:50`;
    - `stat` of the log.
  - **Store:** an append-only `samples.jsonl`. **One row is one readback of one run at one UTC.** The columns are `utc`,
    `monotonic_s`, `run_id`, `latest_complete_iteration`, `log_bytes`, `log_mtime`, `unit_active_state`, `loadavg_1m`
    and `observer_sha256`.
  - **Rate:** Δiteration / Δmonotonic between consecutive rows. "Before", "during" and "after" are medians, aligned to
    the GPU step's `capture.json` times.
  - **Schedule:** every 600 s, from 30 min before to 30 min after each WSL-side GPU step, as a bounded foreground loop
    with no system scheduler entry.
  - **Stop rule:** a 5 % slowdown stops only the GPU track's own processes. The threshold is Inferred as a sensible
    default, and the operator may amend it.
- **(c) G1 is approved, read-only.**
  - **Bounds:** no `wmake` or compiler, no `apt`, no change under `/usr/lib/openfoam`, no system change. The source is
    read on the web or fetched as a SHA-256-recorded archive into the track's own folder. WSL steps run single-process
    under `nice -n 10` with the observer running.
  - **Deliverable:** the F1 bill of materials:
    - the source hash and licence;
    - the offload backend and compiler (unified against discrete memory; Umpire `managed` pools);
    - whether `simpleFoam` with fused schemes reaches offload code;
    - `ldd` of `libfusedFiniteVolume.so` and `libOpenFOAM.so`;
    - the petsc4Foam contract;
    - the amgx4Foam and RapidCFD rows, rejected with a reason.
  - G1 does not authorise F1.
