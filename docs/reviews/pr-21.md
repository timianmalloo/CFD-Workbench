## Findings

1. **The run meets Ruling 177.** The tested head contains WDF and not DPR. `P6_SCALE_CONTEXT RenderScaling=1.5
   PrimaryScaling=1.5` precedes each 150 % check, and 2.0/2.0 each 200 % check. There is no AppliedDPI predicate.
2. **Item 8 is closed on Windows.** It was red before WDF (PR #20) and is green on a WDF head at 1.5 and 2.0. Items 1
   and 7 pass at 1.5. One gap: the ninth named check, `PropertiesPane_Density_EveryInputDeclaresMinHeightOf24`, was not
   run.
3. **Item 2 is a second assertion.** `Near(width, grid.Bounds.Width, 0, "arrangement width")` has zero tolerance
   (ControllerViewTests.cs:622 at the tested head, :640 on main). 647 DIP lays out at 971 px = 647.333 DIP. WDF changed
   only the later `AssertFourOrOne`. The product's fall-back to one view is correct (Inferred from arithmetic).
4. **Item 3** fails exactly as the pre-DPR expectation predicts. DPR's repair is on main.
5. **Items 4 and 5.**
   - At a 96-dpi capture, the 1 DIP border blends on a 2/3-DIP seam.
   - At device resolution, View3d's chip is present.
   - Everything `ElevationView.RenderOverlay` draws (the Side chips, the lane caption, Front starboard/port) is
     **absent**, on both Windows 1.5 and Mac 2.0, while `Render`'s ground line is present.
   - Elevation wraps its overlay in `PushOpacity` + `PushClip` (ElevationView.cs:1115, :1199). View3d does not.

   Likely a test-capture defect (RenderTargetBitmap at DPI ≠ 96), Inferred. A spike, or an OS screenshot of the live
   window, settles it.
6. **Item 6.** 1.0 DIP is 1.5 device pixels, against a predicted 0.667. The fact-TextBlock print was not delivered.
7. **Captures, PII, scope.** 85/85 manifest entries match, PII is 0, and the diff is docs only. Exit codes are honestly
   recorded as NOT_RECORDED.
