# PR #13 — Fable owner review

PR #13 "proof: record Windows trigonometric bit patterns" (origin/win/trig-bits, reviewed head 24313e2b, evidence commit
b5c6b52e) was reviewed by the Fable owner on the Mac on 2026-10-08 under Ruling 106. Verdict: **APPROVE WITH
CONDITIONS**, issued as **Ruling 163**. The Mac requested this job as the join gate of Ruling 156 (2), and the CAF build
(naca4-closed/2) was joined on this evidence.

## Findings

1. **The gate is met (Verified).** 81 of 81 `double.CosPi(i/80.0)` rows equal `spacing-cospi-80.tsv`. This was checked by
   the reviewer's own parser and independently by the leader. On Windows, `Math.Cos` differs from `CosPi` at 42
   indices, including i = 63 (`…17b1` vs `…17b0`). That is the catalog byte-727 mechanism, measured. Excluding the
   i = 40 zero crossing, the largest difference is 21 ulp. `SinPi` differs from `Math.Sin` at 36 indices.
2. **First direct cross-OS measurement (Verified).** The reviewer ran the identical program on the Mac (arm64, SDK
   10.0.203). The `CosPi` and `SinPi` columns are bit-identical between the two hosts at all 81 indices. `Math.Cos`
   differs at 5 indices (28, 55, 59, 62, 63) and `Math.Sin` at 8.
3. **The program matches the request (Verified).** It has the four columns per i and the four scalar samples, ran in
   Release, and records `dotnet --info`, the OS build (26300) and the CPU in prose, with raw stdout. Program.cs is LF,
   and its SHA-256 matches the receipt.
4. **The receipt is honest (Verified).** It says "single-host measurement, not cross-platform proof", and it makes no
   P5 claim.
5. **`.gitattributes` (Verified).** `bits.stdout.txt -text` keeps the CRLF capture (13,584 bytes). But commit 1f2cc9f8
   had normalised it to LF (13,499 bytes) before b5c6b52e restored it. That is a recurrence of the Ruling 151 C3 class.
   The payload lines are identical, so no number is affected.
6. **Privacy (Verified).** 0 PROOF-PII hits.
7. **Scope (Verified).** Six files under `docs/proof/win-trig-bits/`. The handoff was a PR comment only, with no xmsg.
8. **Docs check (Verified).** It fails at the head: `rel: tests` is unregistered (V14), and the index has drifted.
9. **Inferred.** Per the .NET 10 source, `CosPi` and `SinPi` are managed polynomial kernels. `AtanPi` and `Atan2Pi` are
   `Math.Atan…/Math.PI`, so no deterministic managed arctangent exists.

## Conditions

- **Pre-merge (Mac, at the join):** `rel: tests` becomes `relates-to`, the index is synced, and check-docs is green.
- **Follow-up:** record 1f2cc9f8 as a Ruling 151 C3 instance, with a control that a receipt-declared raw-capture SHA-256
  and byte count equal the committed blob.

## Advice on Ruling 157

The four scalar samples matching is not determinism evidence. `Math.Cos` and `Math.Sin` differ across OS at 6–10 % of
arguments at the 1-ulp level, so `Atan` and `Atan2` must be presumed to differ too. Ruling 157 stands: `SinCosPi` for the
Cos/Sin pairs is now backed by measurement, and the Atan families keep the 1e-6 identity tolerance, measured on the PC
ring.
