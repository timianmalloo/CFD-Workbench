## Findings

1. **Head movement.** cd5a5a49 → e0fd2778 is a merge of main into the branch. The `docs/proof/win-cfmesh/` files are
   byte-identical across the two heads. `xmsg.jsonl` equals main's register plus two new PC entries.

3. **Ruling 102 step 1 is not met.** Step 1 asks whether the *installed OpenFOAM* provides cfMesh. "Not resolved in an
   unactivated shell" tests the shell, not the installation. The receipt agrees (receipt.md:44–46, "installation
   presence is unresolved").

   `/usr/lib/openfoam/openfoam2512/etc/bashrc`. W-3's receipt (win-routes/receipt.md:92) says the bashrc fails under
   `set -e`. Both repair cycles went on PowerShell → wsl → bash quoting instead. The cause is Inferred.
5. **Evidence points to "present".** `cycle-1-console.txt:5` records `command -v cartesianMesh` resolving to
   `.../platforms/linux64GccDPInt32Opt/bin/cartesianMesh` from a separate subcommand (the raw stream was not kept).
   Cycle 2's `command -v` was empty. The fresh probe settles the difference.
6. **Cycle-2 version blank.** Inside a double-quoted `bash -lc` string, bash expands `${Version}` to empty, so dpkg
   printed a blank line. This is Inferred; the receipt reports "no version" without the cause.
7. **Repair cap honest.** probe.md:20–36 records 2/2 cycles and no third attempt.
8. **Privacy.** The PROOF-PII scan over all 9 head files finds 0 hits.
9. **Docs check.** `probe.md` has no `links` (an orphan), and the index lacks `proof-win-cfmesh`. Also, `probe.ps1`
   rewrites `probe.md` without frontmatter on any rerun (probe.ps1:20–35).
10. **L3 contention.** L3 runs six ranks at nice 19 on 14 physical cores, and cycle 2 took 1.23 s. A help probe is
    negligible. This is Inferred; the probe measures loadavg before and after.

## Ruling 153 — fresh availability probe authorized

One availability-only track: a new 2-cycle budget, a 10-minute ceiling, inside Ruling 102 step 1. No install, build,
case YAML, mesh or solver run.

**How it runs:**
- A committed LF `.sh` that reuses W-3's activation route.
- It sources the bashrc with errexit off, then sets `set -u`.
- It records each stage's exit separately (no `&&` chain), and runs under `nice -n 10`.

**What it records:**
- start and end UTC, and the script's sha256;
- `WM_PROJECT_VERSION`, `WM_PROJECT_DIR`, `FOAM_APPBIN`;
- `dpkg-query -W -f='${Package} ${Version} ${Status}\n' openfoam2512`, single-quoted in the file;
- `command -v cartesianMesh`, plus `ls -l` and `sha256sum` of the resolved path;
- `cartesianMesh -help`, with stdout and stderr in separate files and its exit code;
- `/proc/loadavg` before and after.

**Exit evidence is one of three outcomes:**
- **(a) Environment unresolved.** The source fails, or `WM_PROJECT_VERSION` is empty: stop and post a decision request.
- **(b) Absent under Ruling 102.** Activation is good, `command -v` is empty, and `$FOAM_APPBIN/cartesianMesh` does not
  exist: stop and post a decision request.
- **(c) Present.** `-help` exits 0 with the usage banner. Record the banner's build string and the dpkg version, then
  return for a separately frozen S4/S6 coupon release (Ruling 98 W2c criteria, a new pre-registration, L3 keeps
  priority). This ruling does not release the coupon.
