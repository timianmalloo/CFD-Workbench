**FINDINGS:**

- **[Blocker] (Verified) Depth and Froude number are not operating-point inputs anywhere the spec computes a number.** A3 line 88 lists "depth" in the Operating point value object, but A4's operating-conditions paragraph (177–183) defines q, Re = Vc/ν and the water record and never defines p_atm, h, h/c, Fr_h or σ; no story derives Fr_h or h/c; "depth" appears only in ANA-02 ("threshold includes … depth"), ANA-13 (tip-depth hint) and ANA-14 (Cp_min "and fluid pressure/temperature/depth"). No deep-water label rule exists, so a polar result at h/c = 3.8 (32 kn, 0.3 m — inside the goal state) is displayed as current with no envelope statement. *evidence:* KB headline 10 and KB-5 ("depth and Froude number are mandatory operating-point inputs … missing depth makes σ, Fr_h and V_crit Unavailable and the deep-water label is never silently applied"); 07 D5 (required fields); 13 D3; 04 §Operating physics (deep-water asymptote only h/c > 5, effects concentrate h/c < 4, ≈ 17 % CL loss between Fr_h 2 and 5 at h/c 4); re-executed goal-state h/c 3.8–6.3, Fr_h 2.3–9.6. *fix:* A3 Operating point = {V; water snapshot {T, S_A, ρ, ν, p_v, ITTC Rev03 revision}; p_atm (default 101.325 kPa, editable, pinned); h_ref at the named datum **and** h(y) per station derived from the elevation channel and static orientation; α; β when supported; target load W [N] with source; Ncrit pair}; derived read-outs q, Re_ref, Re(y), σ(y), Fr_h, h/c, V_crit. New story **ANA-19 · I can see the depth basis of every result**: Given no depth, then σ, Fr_h, V_crit read Unavailable and no "deep water" label is printed; Given h/c < 5 at any station, then every low-order result carries "Deep-water polar; free surface not modelled (h/c = x, Fr_h = y; effects measured below h/c 5)"; Given h ≤ 0 at any station, then the estimator is Unavailable for that station and only a surface-piercing/ventilation flag is shown. This is the veto predicate; nothing else clears it.

- **[Major] (Verified) The free-surface correction rule is inverted: the spec defers all free-surface capability to CFD, while the KB requires a labelled low-order layer and requires ANA-05 to state its depth basis.** A2 line 63: "Free-surface and ventilation capability requires a separate de-risked, validated extension after initial single-phase CFD." ANA-05 promises "lift = target within 1 %". The depth-only correction at goal-state depths is 1.2–4.5 % (re-executed: factor 0.955–0.988), i.e. larger than the tolerance, so "within 1 %" is not falsifiable until the basis is printed. *evidence:* KB-12 ("ships only as a labelled Computed-estimate layer that carries h/c only … prints Fr_h beside it with 'Froude dependence not modelled', and is never the displayed lift; 'Find operating α' states whether it was applied"); 07 §Free-surface image system (fit constants (0.45, 0.70) lift; validity NACA 63-210, h/c 0.5–9.5, Re 7.3×10⁴–2.9×10⁵, Fr_h ≲ 5; goal state outside the Re range and at the top of Fr_h); 13 headline 5 (K-factor 2–4 % vs measured 17 % Froude delta); 07 D11 (a corrected value is a separate fact, never an overwrite). *fix:* split the A2 sentence into (a) VOF/free-surface **CFD** — deferred, and (b) depth and Fr_h as **v1 inputs** plus an optional **Computed estimate · depth-only correction** layer (JMSA 2026 fit or infinite-Fr K-factor) shown beside, never instead of, the deep-water value, labelled with (h/c, Fr_h, correction id, source, "model-scale Re 10⁵ data", "Froude dependence not modelled"). ANA-05 and ANA-12 add: "the result names its depth basis: *deep-water, uncorrected* or *correction <id> applied*, and shows h/c and Fr_h."

- **[Major] (Verified) Ncrit is specified as a constant 2.0; the evidence supports a pair run and shown together.** ANA-15: "A named water-screening Ncrit preset initially proposes 2.0, visibly labeled a source-based assumption." *evidence:* KB-9; KB headline 15 ("1 < Ncrit < 3" traces to a 2004 forum post that disclaims test data; Day 2019 tank comparison used Ncrit 4; band 2–4 as a pair); 06 D7 (label text "practitioner range; no measured water N-factor; Day 2019 used 4"); 06 failure modes (air Ncrit 9 shows a laminar bucket that does not exist at sea; Ncrit 1 removes buckets that may exist). *fix:* ANA-15: "polars are computed at the Ncrit pair {2, 4} and displayed as a band labelled 'practitioner range; no measured water N-factor; Day 2019 used 4'; a single-Ncrit polar is admissible only with an explicit user override recorded in the run." ANA-01, ANA-10, ANA-14 and ANA-16 legends carry both values; the polar cache/fact key includes Ncrit (already implied by "changing Ncrit invalidates the polar").

- **[Major] (Verified) No polar carries a surface state; all polars are silently clean-surface.** The spec never mentions transition trip, roughness or surface state. *evidence:* KB headline 31 and KB-9 ("XFOIL polars are clean-surface by construction … Braslow Re_k ≈ 250–600 means a 0.3 mm ding trips the layer at sport speeds, so polars must carry a surface state and show clean-versus-tripped as a band"); 12 via index. *fix:* the polar fact row and the ANA-01/ANA-14 legend carry `surface_state ∈ {clean, tripped(x_trip or k_s)}`; the analysis summary shows clean and tripped as a band or reads "tripped: Unavailable (not computed)"; the fully-turbulent friction bound (07 §Fully turbulent fallback) is the estimator tier's drag label.

- **[Major] (Verified) The ventilation statement is incomplete: no Fr_h beside the tip-depth hint, no fixed text, no surface-piercing state.** ANA-13: "tip-depth hint derives from actual geometry and is labeled Static geometry, no wave/free-surface prediction; missing depth yields Unavailable." - **[Major] (Verified) Seawater handling has the right rule but no control that would catch the silent failure.** A4 line 183 forbids the density-only multiplier and ANA-15 requires recomputation — correct — but no criterion states the defect signature. *evidence:* KB headline 11 (ν_sea/ν_fresh 1.044 → Re −4.3 % at 15 °C; 10 → 30 °C moves Re ×1.6–1.9; "must go through the polar"); re-executed 1.0444 / 4.25 % / ρ ratio 1.0269; 04 failure modes ("silent — applying a density multiplier for salt water without re-evaluating Re"). *fix:* add a fixture to ANA-15: "Given 15 °C, fresh → salt at fixed V and c, then Re decreases by 4.25 % (±0.05) and the coefficients are re-retrieved at the new Re; a result whose loads change by exactly ρ_sea/ρ_fresh = 1.0269 with unchanged Re-dependent coefficients fails." **FINDINGS:**

- **[Major] (Verified) The user's goal state cannot be expressed: there is no Goal state value object, and ANA-05/ANA-12 accept one bare target load at one speed.** The user's example (90 kg rider, race wingfoil, salt water, 10–20 kn wind) resolves to ≈ 105 kg system, W ≈ 1030 N, front-wing load ≈ 1050 N, and seven operating points (1000 cm²/AR 12: CL 0.773/0.395/0.239 at 10/14/18 kn; 750 cm²/AR 12: CL 0.458/0.213/0.132/0.101 at 15/22/28/32 kn; Re 3.9×10⁵–1.1×10⁶; σ 7.7–0.75), with every speed Flagged (no race GPS dataset).
