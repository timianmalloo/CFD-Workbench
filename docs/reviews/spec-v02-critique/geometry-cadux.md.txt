# Critique — specification revision 0.2, geometry and marine-CAD-UX lenses

**Artifact:** `/Users/mallalieut/projects/CFD-Workbench-feature-knowledge-experts-spec-v1/docs/specs/cfd-workbench.md` (rev 0.2, 592 lines).
**Mockup opened:** `docs/mockups/workbench.html` (143 lines, minified; grepped for the editor controls, not rendered).
**Evidence read:** KB index (full, 31 headlines, contradiction register, KB-1…KB-24); area 02 (full); area 03 (headlines, root-mirror paragraph, implications); area 01 (headlines, implications, nudge table, per-OS table, harness checks); area 05 (DAT layouts, STEP shell, JCS/round-trip rows); area 06 (implications, admission list).
**Line references** are to the spec file as read this session (A3 table lines 73–107; A4 lines 111–173; A5 stories DOC-02 l.206, GEO-01…14 l.209–222, CAT-01…03 l.223–225, EXP-01/02 l.254–255; B7 l.469–472; Part C UI-03/04/11 §C3). Every line number was read this session; section ids are given beside them so a re-flowed file still resolves.

Verdict summary: the spec's *intent* matches the KB on every headline (five channels, two modes, one authority, normalized blend, no silent relaxation). What is missing is the **content of the record** and the **measurement contracts** that make the intent testable — and the mockup implements a different mathematics from the one the spec's own criteria imply.

- **[Blocker] (Verified) The A3 model cannot reproduce the surface.** A3 l.80 defines *Distribution curve* as "mode, controls and explicit constraints"; l.81 *Curve control* as "position and finite positive influence weight"; l.86 *Loft rule* as "correspondence, interpolation, continuity and closure intent". No entry carries degree, the clamped knot vector, explicit (all-ones) weights, the attachment parameter of each control, the parameterization choice, the fairness functional and λ, the blend rule id, the loft rule id, or the evaluator id/version. GEO-14 l.222 ("controls, weights, mode, locks and evaluated shape recover within DOC-02 tolerance") is untestable without a pinned evaluator, because the Smooth solution depends on the functional and λ. **evidence:** KB-1; area 02 §8 payload and rule (2) "evaluator.version is part of the record". **fix:** A3 rows — *Distribution curve* = family (clamped non-rational B-spline), degree 5, knots (count convention stated), control points, weights (explicit, all 1), mode, per-mode intent (anchors+tangents | controls+weights+attach parameter), constraint rows, continuity intent, provenance; *Surface revision* additionally carries evaluator {id, version, solver, fairness, λ}, tolerances (three named), blend {id, version}, loft {rule, v-degree, parameterization}, correspondence. Add a criterion: "the saved definition re-evaluates on both platforms to the pre-save geometry within the identity tolerance; a mismatch is an evaluator change and creates a revision".

- **[Blocker] (Verified) The loft rule is left to the exporter.** A4 l.113 ("Estimation, meshing and export shall evaluate that same definition") implicitly chooses rule A (channel-evaluated analytic surface), which is *not* a tensor-product B-spline in η once twist(η) rotates the section; EXP-02 l.255 requires STEP "open-and-measure proof" but never a measured deviation of the exported skin from the record, and A3 *Loft rule* names no rule id. **evidence:** KB-3, KB-5; area 02 §5 "Channel-driven loft vs skinning", implication 6, headline 10; area 03 headline 8 (MultiSurf's "very close approximation… one way street"). **fix:** A3 *Loft rule* carries `rule ∈ {channel-evaluated (A), skin-station-nets (B)}` and the spec commits to A in words; EXP-02 gains "any B-spline skin (STEP, 3DM, viewport) is a derived approximation built by sampling until its maximum deviation from the record ≤ model tolerance; the deviation and tolerance are shown in the export dialog and written into the file's provenance"; the A-vs-B deviation fixture (area 02 Q3: washout 2°, taper 0.35, 4 stations) is a named ring-0 fixture.

- **[Major] (Verified) DOC-02 stores evaluated geometry.** l.206: "the full explicit definition, **evaluated geometry**, editing state and provenance survive within 1 µm". Persisting evaluated coordinates beside the definition creates the second authority the persona's veto names (coordinates-as-record; GEO-A/GEO-B). **evidence:** KB-1 "never store evaluated coordinates, span, area or AR"; area 02 §8 rule (1). **fix:** DOC-02 reads "the explicit definition, editing state and provenance survive; on reopen the evaluated geometry, area, span and AR are *recomputed* and equal the pre-save values within the identity tolerance (1 µm absolute and 10⁻⁶ relative, both must fail to differ)". Nothing evaluated is written except as a labelled cache that is discarded on hash mismatch.

- **[Major] (Verified) One tolerance number where three are needed.** The spec uses 1 µm for identity (DOC-02, GEO-06 l.214, GEO-11 l.219 — correct), reporting (GEO-01 l.209 "1 µm/relative 10⁻⁶" — correct), and then leaves "geometry tolerance" (A4 l.127 promotion), "declared evaluator tolerance" (l.143 continuity) and "declared tolerance" (GEO-13 l.221 locks) unnamed. There is no model/join tolerance and no angle tolerance anywhere. A reader will reuse 1 µm operationally (closest-point, join, self-intersection) — the failure area 02 warns about. **evidence:** KB-1 three tolerances; area 02 §6 "Is 1 µm sensible?", implication 9, disconfirming view 5. **fix:** A4 "Coordinates, dimensions and units" states three named tolerances — identity 1 µm ∧ 10⁻⁶ relative; model/join 10 µm (user-visible, Rhino-style); angle 0.1° — and a rule: the identity tolerance is never a convergence or join criterion. Each criterion names which tolerance it uses.

- **[Major] (Verified) "G2 within a smoothed region" measures the wrong place and is not falsifiable.** A4 l.143: "Continuity within a smoothed region shall be at least G2 to a declared evaluator tolerance". Inside one degree-5 curve with simple knots G2 (indeed C⁴) is automatic; the real G2 risks are the LE junction of upper and lower curves, the mirror plane at the root, profile transitions along η, split handles and any approximate refit. No one-sided measurement, no point/angle/curvature tolerance triple, no junction list, and no rule that deliberate breaks are stored as knot multiplicity. **evidence:** KB-4 (headline 4); area 02 §1 "What degree 5 gives G4 precisely means" (four failure cases), §1 comb paragraph (one-sided de Boor evaluation, openNURBS `IsContinuous` pattern), implication 4. **fix:** criterion: "At every interior knot and at each named junction (upper/lower at LE, root mirror plane, each profile transition, each split handle) the evaluator computes one-sided G0/G1/G2 gaps and compares them to (identity distance, 0.1°, curvature tolerance); the preview lists every gap above tolerance; deliberate breaks are stored as `continuity_intent` (knot multiplicity) and shown as intent, not defects."

- **[Major] (Verified) Insertion and removal are treated symmetrically; degree is "where used".** A4 l.149: "Insertion/removal preserves degree-five representation where used and reports any change in shape". Knot insertion is exact (executed 6.9×10⁻¹⁷); knot removal and degree reduction are approximations. "Where used" leaves the family undecided while GEO-06's 1 µm test depends on it. No Schoenberg–Whitney / "insufficient controls" test is named before a solve. **evidence:** KB-1, KB-23; area 02 headline 2, §1 "Exactness of representation changes", implication 5, invariants list. **fix:** split the criterion — insertion (control or station) "reports a change of exactly zero (≤ identity tolerance)"; removal "reports the measured deviation and is blocked above the model tolerance unless accepted"; the family is degree 5 clamped non-rational by A3, not "where used"; every interpolation/LSQ solve tests Schoenberg–Whitney first and rejects with "insufficient controls" (GEO-05 deletion block gets its mathematical reason).

- **[Major] (Verified) The Smooth formulation is unstated, and the mockup implements the rejected one.** A4 l.141 leaves "curve family/evaluator" open, yet GEO-13 asserts behaviours (weight 1→2 attracts without reaching; "affected curve region"; "a finite weight never creates a hard lock") that hold only for specific formulations. The mockup's `rational(values,weights,t)` is a **degree-4 rational Bézier** in η with NURBS weights on **five fixed controls**: global support (every weight moves the whole curve, contradicting "affected region"), rational (breaks polynomial STEP export), no constraint mechanism, no insertion. **evidence:** KB-2, KB-3 (headline 3: "Rational NURBS weights are rejected for shape"); area 02 §2(b) table and recommended formulation; mockup source `function rational(...)`, `b=[u**4,4*u**3*t,…]`, `weights:[1,1,1,1,1]`. **fix:** A4 states the formulation as a product decision, not a stack decision: "Smooth = weighted least squares on a fixed knot vector with hard constraints as equality rows; weights are data-fit weights, relative, w→∞ converges to interpolation; NURBS weights are stored explicitly as 1 and never used for shape; Through points is the zero-residual special case of the same solver." C4 relabels the mockup evaluator "illustrative; not the record formulation" until it is replaced.

- **[Major] (Verified) Constraint rows have no type catalogue and no source.** A3 l.82 lists "endpoint, evaluated value, tangent or symmetry"; A4 l.145 lists locks to preserve. Missing row types: curvature match at the LE junction, closure, root-mirror tangent, axis-locked tangent, fixed-angle tangent, value-at-station (GEO-03 names it but A3 does not), recipe driver. No row carries its **source**, so "Conflicting locks … identify the cause" (l.145) cannot name both parties. **evidence:** KB-2; area 02 §8 `constraints: [{type…, source}]` and rule (3); area 03 implication 4 (Shape3d's five tangent kinds → constraint types). **fix:** A3 *Curve constraint* = {type ∈ the enumerated set, parameter (u or η), value, source ∈ {user lock, symmetry, closure, station value, recipe driver}}; conflicts are reported as rank/consistency failures naming the rows; never relaxed.

- **[Major] (Verified) The root-mirror tangent invariant is absent.** A4 l.145 preserves "mirror relationships" and GEO-11 l.219 requires "both halves agree" — both are G0 statements. A mirrored wing whose chord, thickness, twist, elevation or sweep channel has df/dy ≠ 0 at y = 0 passes both criteria with a kink across the root, which Break symmetry then inherits invisibly. **evidence:** area 03 "Root-mirror tangent invariant (derived)" and implication 4; KB-2 lists root-mirror tangent among the hard rows. **fix:** default hard constraint df/dη = 0 at η = 0 on all five channels while mirrored (a named, visible lock, releasable only with Break symmetry); GEO-11 adds "the surface is G1 across the symmetry plane (tangent angle ≤ 0.1°) before and after Break symmetry". Label `assume:` until fixtured, as the area file does.

- **[Major] (Verified) Thickness is applied "about" the camber line — the rule that decides exactness is unstated.** A4 l.125 "Symmetric thickness scaling about the normalized camber line"; l.129 "camber and a unit-thickness shape". NACA 4-digit applies thickness *normal* to the camber line; the KB blend rule blends at equal ψ (vertical offset). Which one the record uses decides whether a cambered NACA 4412 is exact or a conversion with residual, and whether "t/c" means the max of (y_u − y_l) or the max normal thickness. **evidence:** area 02 §4 NACA paragraph, contradiction register row "cambered 4-digit… not polynomial"; index register row 5. **fix:** A4 states: "thickness is the vertical (ψ-aligned) half-difference between upper and lower at equal ψ; t/c is its maximum; catalog sections whose definition applies thickness normal to camber are conversions and report their residual".

- **[Major] (Verified) Conversion residuals are reported for one path only.** A4 l.139 requires deviation for the *Edit section* draft. Not covered: (a) catalog assignment with *Use source thickness* — GEO-07 l.215 says "endpoint geometry matches them" with no tolerance and no residual of the coordinates→B-spline fit; (b) NACA generation — l.163 "admitted NACA 64A410" with no statement that 6/6A-series are coordinates with TM 4741 provenance (~5×10⁻⁵ c), never a formula; (c) the exporter's DAT precision. **evidence:** KB-7, KB-8; area 02 headline 15, implication 8; area 06 implication 5 (as-filed vs normalized geometry, TE closure, resampling as revision attributes); area 05 §DAT (AeroSandbox writes 6 decimals = 1 µm at c = 1, no margin). **fix:** every *Profile revision* carries `conversion {method, max_dev_normalized, tolerance, accepted_by}` plus as-filed bytes+hash, detected layout, normalization transform, TE closure applied, resampling; NACA 6/6A entries record generator id+hash and the TM 4741 residual; DAT export writes shortest-round-trip digits and states its resolution in the EXP-02 dialog.

- **[Major] (Verified) STEP is under-specified for a CAM reader.** EXP-02 l.255 gates release on "open-and-measure proof" but names no entity, no topology (bare surface vs face/shell/solid), no unit (mm), no global uncertainty, no degenerate-tip rule, no reported skin deviation. **evidence:** KB-15; area 05 headline 6 ("CAM systems want a face/shell/solid, not a bare surface"), §STEP units paragraph; area 02 Q6 (degenerate-tip acceptance), §6 degenerate cases; index register row on OpenVSP trimmed vs untrimmed. **fix:** EXP-02: "STEP = `B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS` surfaces bound into faces and a closed shell (solid when closure allows), millimetres, `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT` = model tolerance, non-rational only (weights all 1 proven), zero-chord tip written as a proper cap or left open and labelled; the skin's measured deviation from the record is shown; release after open-and-measure in ≥ 2 named CAM systems".

- **[Major] (Verified) Blend and correspondence have no parameterization.** A4 l.151 "align leading edge, trailing edge, upper and lower surfaces at normalized chord locations" — no shared knot vector, no fixed u at TE-upper/LE/TE-lower, no spacing rule, no feature-alignment option, and the equal-control-count rule is neither adopted nor rejected. The "different control counts at root and tip" fixture is missing (only the thickness-peak fixture exists). **evidence:** KB-3; area 02 §5 "Our lever: compatibility by construction", headline 9; area 03 headline 9 (BoardCAD S-blend vs point correspondence), implication 8. **fix:** A4: "Every profile is fitted once onto a shared degree-5 knot vector with u = 0 at TE-upper, u = u_LE (fixed) at LE, u = 1 at TE-lower, cosine-like spacing in ψ; feature alignment (max-thickness) is a reparameterization before fitting; sections are therefore skinning-compatible with zero knot merging; different control counts at root and tip is a mandatory fixture; the equal-count slice rule is explicitly not adopted."

- **[Minor] (Verified) Integrals have no exactness or error contract.** A4 l.173 defines S and names wetted area/volume; GEO-02 gives closed-form checks. No statement of per-span Gauss–Legendre exactness for S (polynomial chord channel) or of a reported quadrature error for wetted area/volume (square-root integrand). **evidence:** area 02 §5 "Exact integrals", implication 11; KB-23. **fix:** "S is exact (per-span Gauss–Legendre of the polynomial chord channel, order stated); wetted area and volume show the rule order and a Richardson error as ±; never a plausible number."

- **[Minor] (Verified) Zero-chord tip is conflated with invalid chord.** A4 l.145 rejects "nonpositive chord"; l.153 offers tip closure; a declared zero-chord tip is legal and common, and needs tessellation collapse and limit normals. **evidence:** area 02 invariants ("chord(η) > 0 except an explicitly declared zero-chord tip"), §6 degenerate cases, implication 12. **fix:** distinguish "chord ≤ 0 in the interior (rejected)" from "declared zero-chord tip (legal; normals as limits; tessellator collapses the edge; STEP cap rule)".

- **[Minor] (Verified) Serialization can consume the identity tolerance.** DOC-02 and the hashes in A3/A5 say nothing about shortest-round-trip doubles, hashing over a canonical form rather than saved bytes, `null` vs NaN, or the knot-count convention. **evidence:** KB-14; area 05 headline 12 (bit-exact round trip, not JCS canonical), §native format rules. **fix:** one A4 sentence: "storage adds zero geometric error (shortest round-trip); the revision hash is over a canonical form of the definition, not the file bytes; the knot-count convention is stated in the format".

- **[Minor] (Inferred) Through-points parameterization and end conditions unstated.** A4 l.141 "on-curve anchors and linked/split tangent direction/magnitude". Degree 5 interpolation needs four extra conditions; chord vs centripetal parameter choice changes the knots; a save/load that re-derives knots differently from the same anchors is a silent shape change; tangent magnitude is parametric speed and should be displayed relative to local chord. **evidence:** area 02 §2(a), failure modes ("parameterization mismatch… between save and load"), §8 rule (4). **fix:** store anchors *and* resulting knots/controls; record the parameterization and end conditions; display handle magnitude relative to local chord.

- **[Nit] (Verified) "Fair" is used without its operational definition.** A4 l.141 "continuously faired"; l.151 "Do not infer fairness from smooth shading" (good). **evidence:** area 02 headline 6 (Farin–Sapidis: few monotone pieces of the curvature plot). **fix:** define fair in A3/glossary and display the monotone-piece count per curve.

- **[Major] (Verified) The mockup's Smooth controls *are* the stations — the anchor-that-is-an-influence anti-pattern the spec forbids.** A4 l.149: "a control's selection and a station's evaluated location are related but never falsely drawn as the same point in Smooth mode"; B1: "the evaluated station marker and influence control marker have separate shapes and labels". Mockup: `sample(eta,key)` in smooth mode returns `rational(a.map(p=>p[key]),def.weights,eta)` where `a` is the **station list** and `def.weights` is keyed by **station index** (`weights[S.selected]`, `S.selected` set by `selectStation`); the Bernstein abscissae ignore station η entirely. So the user drags a station, its readout is a rational evaluation at that station's η, and the "control" is the station's own value. The editor teaches a model the record does not have (False-CAD-Model). **evidence:** mockup source; area 03 implication 3 ("its inspector must say 'influence, not anchor' every time"); area 01 headline 2. **fix:** C4: the prototype must show off-curve controls at their own abscissae, distinct glyphs for control vs evaluated station, and a dashed **control polygon** (the mockup's dashed strokes are gridlines, the ghost and the before-shape — UI-11's polygon is not drawn).

- **[Major] (Verified) "Quintic preview" label over a quartic rational Bézier and a C¹ Hermite.** Mockup prints "Interpolation · Quintic preview" and "Quintic preview · five channels" while Through points evaluates a per-segment cubic Hermite with stored slopes (C¹) and Smooth evaluates degree-4 rational Bézier. - **[Major] (Verified) Two nudge steps where experts expect three, and no weight nudge.** A4 l.149 "1 mm default positional nudge and 0.1 mm fine nudge… angle/ratio and normalized-section steps separately visible". ## TIGHTEN (criteria that are not falsifiable, terms with two meanings, claims stronger than the evidence)

1. **"Continuity within a smoothed region shall be at least G2 to a declared evaluator tolerance"** (A4 l.143) — not falsifiable; replace with the per-knot/per-junction one-sided measurement against the named triple.
2. **"Insertion/removal preserves degree-five representation where used and reports any change in shape"** (l.149) — "where used" undecided; insertion is exact, removal is not; split and pin the family.
3. **"within geometry tolerance" / "declared tolerance" / "1 µm"** — one number doing three jobs; name identity/model/angle per criterion and forbid the identity tolerance operationally.
4. **DOC-02 "evaluated geometry … survive"** — store the definition, recompute and compare; never persist evaluated geometry as record.
5. **"Symmetric thickness scaling about the normalized camber line"** (l.125) — "about" has two meanings (normal vs vertical); pick one and state the residual consequence.
6. **"Blend correspondence shall align LE, TE, upper and lower surfaces at normalized chord locations"** (l.151) — state the shared parameterization and the fixed u values.
7. **"nonpositive chord … rejected"** (l.145) — distinguish interior chord ≤ 0 from a declared zero-chord tip.
8. **GEO-07 "endpoint geometry matches them"** — to what tolerance, and what residual for the catalog→B-spline fit?
9. **GEO-11 "both halves agree"** — G0 only; require G1 across the root (angle tolerance).
10. **GEO-13 "changes toward that control"** — state the formulation whose property this is (constrained weighted LSQ; convex; residual strictly decreasing in w with others fixed), else it is a hope.
11. **"Smooth" and "Fair"** are used for one manual mode; reserve *Fair* for the automatic tolerance-bounded action and define fair operationally (few monotone curvature pieces).
12. **"Through points preserves … tangent direction/magnitude"** — magnitude is parametric speed; display relative to local chord and store the parameterization.
13. **"A general expression language is not required in v1"** (l.135) — replace the negative with the positive Onshape rule (unit-suffixed literal, named dimension reference, arithmetic, first-power family, echo).
14. **"1 mm default positional nudge and 0.1 mm fine nudge"** — three steps per unit family; weight step multiplicative.
15. **B1 "a command search lists commands with Mac/Windows shortcuts"** — the shortcuts do not exist in the spec; the per-OS table must be normative before this sentence is true.
16. **B7 "curvature comb with scale"** — eight properties, not one phrase.
17. **EXP-02 "STEP release requires target CAD/CAM open-and-measure proof"** — name the topology, unit, uncertainty, deviation and reader count so "proof" has a pass condition.
18. **C4 "The prototype now demonstrates weighted curve edits"** (frontmatter review note) — it demonstrates a rational Bézier over station values with a "Quintic" label and no comb; relabel until the prototype matches the record semantics.
19. **"open/sharp/finite-thickness trailing-edge and tip/root closure choices"** (l.153) — adopt `None / Skew lower / Skew upper / Skew both / Extrapolate` and `trim by x / by thickness` so exporter, XFLR5 and OpenVSP users share words.
20. **"Manufacturing assessed … Not assessed"** (l.153) — keep; but the TE-floor policy value GEO-12 consumes is the one structural field modelled in v1 (KB-18) — say so, so the reader-trace rule is visibly honoured.
